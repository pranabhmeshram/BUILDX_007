# RAKSHA-SETU 360 (सुरक्षा सेतु)
## Next-Gen Public Safety, Smart Crowd AI & Emergency Coordination Ecosystem

A unified web platform designed to eliminate single-point failures in public space safety, mass gatherings (such as *Dhammachakra Pravartan Din* at Deekshabhoomi, Nagpur), and everyday citizen transit.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules & Features

### 1. 🚨 Interactive Crisis Sandbox (Deekshabhoomi Replay & Urban Scenarios)
- **5:40 PM Deekshabhoomi Timeline**: Step-by-step interactive simulation resolving the 6-year-old lost child (Aaradhya Meshram, Chandrapur) crisis in under 15 minutes.
- **Synchronized Digital Helpdesks**: Replaces the 25-minute paper notebook lag with immediate multi-desk peer-to-peer cloud sync.
- **Privacy-Preserving Volunteer Push**: Cryptographically watermarked photo alerts dispatched exclusively to verified NSS & police tablets, completely eliminating unverified social media chaos and false sightings.
- **Pre-emptive Crowd Surge Rerouting**: Real-time optical velocity and crowd density gauges at Exit 2 (89%) and Exit 4 (94%) trigger automated LED dynamic signage and green corridor diversion to Exit 3.
- **Zoned PA Multilingual Loudspeaker**: Localized audio alerts broadcasted in Marathi, Hindi, and English to targeted zones via Web Audio & Speech API.

### 2. 🌐 Integrated Command & Control Center (ICCC / Suraksha HQ)
- Tactical GIS Dark-Themed Map of Deekshabhoomi with real-time crowd heatmaps, exit bottlenecks, and synchronized helpdesks.
- 6-Channel Live AI CCTV Matrix with real-time computer vision bounding boxes and confidence score match overlays.
- VoIP / Digital Radio Dispatch matrix with categorized channels.

### 3. 🛡️ First Responder & Field Helpdesk Station
- Synchronized Multi-Desk Registry across 8 field posts.
- Fast Missing Person Registration with visual descriptor tagging.
- Parent Handover Verification with SMS handshake OTP.

### 4. 📱 Citizen Suraksha Companion (PWA Mobile Experience)
- **SafeRide Guardian**: Real-time GPS corridor tracking with 150m deviation alerts, auto SOS countdown, and realistic fake incoming call shield (*"Inspector Dad Calling"*).
- **Anonymous Whistleblower Reporter**: 1-tap zero-friction reporting for chain snatching and harassment with automated CCTV timestamp locking.
- **Silver Alert & QR Lifeline**: QR emergency badges and localized auto-rickshaw union broadcasts for wandering elderly relatives.

### 5. 🏗️ Tech Blueprint & Comparative Impact Matrix
- Before vs. After Comparative Matrix detailing 97% time reduction in missing person intake and pre-emptive stampede prevention.
- Stage-by-stage Stampede Prevention SOP (Detection, Diversion, Dispatch).

Simply launch the built-in server:
